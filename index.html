The short version

In the Expected case the 5-year NPV is +⁠$1.18M, ROI is 42%, and payback comes in at about 22 months. Worker time, supervisor time and a small turnover line supply about 63% of the steady-state value, so the result depends on them.

Plant HR capacity plus avoided incidents come to −⁠$1.57M over five years and never pay back. Covering the platform cost with HR capacity alone takes 7.3 FTE freed at 100% cash conversion (33% of the 22 plant HR staff), or 10.4 FTE at 70% realization (47%).

A target of two fewer HR staff per plant is 8 FTE, or 36% of plant HR. That is worth $0.56M to $0.80M a year against a $726K annual platform cost. It roughly breaks even at best (hard-only NPV of −⁠$0.93M at 70% realization and −⁠$0.09M at 100%), so it cannot carry the case alone.

License size moves the number most. The base case licenses 300 HR Service Desk seats and 10,000 platform users, which is $510K a year. Scaled to 6,000 users and about 24 seats, it comes to about $228K a year, and the Expected total NPV rises to +⁠$2.31M. That is arithmetic on unit prices, so the figure needs pricing confirmation before anyone quotes it.

The Conservative case does not pay back (total NPV −⁠$1.46M). The Aggressive case returns +⁠$3.64M with payback in about 8 months, although it is still negative on hard savings alone (−⁠$0.58M).

What is modeled

Plants
Four US plants: Kenworth Chillicothe (OH), Kenworth Renton (WA), Peterbilt Denton (TX) and PACCAR Engine Company Columbus (MS). The list comes from public research and is unconfirmed.
Plant HR staff
22 full-time staff (about 5.5 per plant) at $100K loaded cost each.
Workforce
About 6,000 hourly union workers and 240 supervisors. Access is phone only on the worker's own device, with Entra ID sign-in. Answers are deterministic and cite policy.
Out of scope
Canada and Mexico, where the workers are salaried.
Time frame
Five years after a Year 0 implementation. Adoption ramps 60%, 85% and then 100% from Year 3. Payback is measured from the Year 0 investment.

Open items

  • Salaried staff. Whether plant HR also supports salaried plant staff. If it does, less of its time is open to hourly-worker self-service than the model assumes.
  • Plant list. PACCAR needs to confirm the four plants. Each plant added or removed moves total NPV by about $0.33M.
  • Pricing. License sizing and the Slack Frontline price are working figures until pricing is confirmed.
